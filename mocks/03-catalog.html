Reading a dial
●●●○○ Filled pips are states already spent. Public on every construct, always.

Dials never regress. No healing, no repair, no state recovery inside a match.

Advancing past the last state destroys the construct — which is why a 4-state spike frame is a countdown you can read from across the board.

Def modifies incoming damage before the floor and the minimum-1 rule are applied.
