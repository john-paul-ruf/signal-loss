4 · Glossary

15 TERMS · DEEP-LINKED FROM EVERY TOOLTIP
Construct
One chassis plus the mounts loaded into it. The thing that moves, fires and dies. Cost = chassis + mounts + commander tag.
Chassis
The frame. Fixes point cost, movement allowance, footprint, hardpoint layout and the dial. Seven exist; all are available from match one.
Mount
A module occupying exactly one hardpoint of its own type. Contributes damage, range, or a modifier — every contributed stat is printed in the Codex.
Hardpoint
A typed port: ICE · DAEMON · SPIKE · SPOOFER · WIPE. Holds at most one mount and may be left empty. A mismatch is refused with its reason stated.
Dial
The ordered damage states of a chassis. Each state redefines move, damage, range and defence. It never regresses — there is no healing — and advancing past the last state destroys the construct.
Commander
The one construct per roster carrying a commander tag. Public to all five squads from round 1. Supplies commander_base to the pool; its death collapses that pool permanently.
Reaction pool
Your per-round budget for postures and called shots. Refills at the start of every round and carries nothing over. Typically 2–5 points against 3–9 constructs.
Posture
A defensive stance declared for 1 point. Takes 0 from an incoming normal shot and half from an incoming called shot. Drawn as brackets ⌐ ¬ clamped around the marker once revealed.
Flat
The default state: undeclared, free, undecorated. Takes full damage from a normal shot and 1.5× from a called shot. Most of your squad is flat most rounds.
Called shot
A shot declared for 1 point. 1.5× into a flat target, 0.5× into a postured one. The guess half of the matching-pennies decision.
Normal shot
The free shot every armed construct may take. Full damage into a flat target, nothing at all into a postured one.
Trace
The closing boundary. Deals escalating damage at the trace step of each round on a schedule that is public from round 1. It overlays terrain rather than deleting it.
Resolution loss
A construct beyond your resolution range renders as a ghost at its last confirmed position, with a drift ring bounding where it could be. Position confidence only — stats, dial and commander status stay fully readable.
Integrity
The damage a construct absorbs inside one dial state. Shot damage and trace damage spend the same pool; exhausting it advances the dial by one state.
Placement
Your finishing rank, 1st through 5th, determined by elimination order. The match ends when one squad remains, or the moment your last construct is destroyed.

Every term above appears in-product as a dotted-underline trigger that opens this drawer at its definition. Intent is the only hidden information in the game — everything else in here is a fact you are entitled to compute.
